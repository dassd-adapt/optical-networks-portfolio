## Professional Summary

I am an experimental photonics researcher with over nine years of hands-on
experience in advanced optical and wireless communication systems. I received
my Ph.D. from Dublin City University in 2024, focused on next-generation
hybrid optical and wireless systems for converged access networking. I am
currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Trinity College Dublin, directing
and delivering key experimental activities within the Horizon Europe SNS-JU
ECO-eNET consortium.

My research has been shaped by a consistent goal: to advance photonic
integration and wireless-optical convergence from fundamental experimentation
through to real-world network deployment. In 2021, I experimentally validated
an InP-Si3N4 integrated dual tunable laser module, demonstrating unamplified
28 GBd PAM-8 transmission across a 100 nm tuning range — work published in
Optics Express that directly supported the commercialisation pathway of a
silicon photonics product. Building on this, I demonstrated a wavelength and
mm-wave flexible ARoF fronthaul architecture in 2022, published in the Journal
of Lightwave Technology and shortlisted for best student paper at ECOC and MWP.
In 2023, I demonstrated a low-noise ultra-flexible Si-PIC switching platform
supporting mmWave OCDM and multi-band OFDM ARoF simultaneously, published in
IEEE Photonics Technology Letters. In 2024, I presented an invited talk at the
Optica Advanced Photonics Congress on hybrid digital PAM and 60 GHz ARoF for
OSaaS applications. This body of work culminated in 2025 with the world's first
demonstration of ARoF transmission over 77 km of commercial fibre, achieving
spectral coexistence of digital coherent, mmWave, and sub-THz ARoF services on
a live production metro-access network — recognised as a Top-Scored paper at
OFC 2025 with an invited journal extension in JOCN.
