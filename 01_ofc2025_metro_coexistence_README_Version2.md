# World-First ARoF over 77 km — OFC 2025

**Coexistence of Digital Coherent, mmWave and sub-THz ARoF Services using
OSaaS over a Converged Access-Metro Live Production Network**

OFC 2025 — Top-Scored Paper
Invited Journal Extension: Journal of Optical Communications and Networking, 2025
Open Access Available

---

## Research Challenge

The core question addressed in this work was whether digital coherent optical
(DCO), millimetre-wave (mmWave), and sub-THz analog radio-over-fiber (ARoF)
services could coexist spectrally and operationally on a real, live commercial
production metro-access network — without compromising signal integrity or
network flexibility.

---

## Approach

- Designed the end-to-end heterogeneous network architecture
- Implemented Optical Spectrum as a Service (OSaaS) control with automatic
  equalisation for multi-service spectral coexistence
- Developed a custom OFDM transceiver in MATLAB for ARoF signal generation
  and reception
- Performed adjacent-channel interference and signal integrity analysis
- Executed all experiments on 77 km of live commercial fibre
- Led manuscript preparation, figure creation, and journal extension

---

## Key Results

- World's first ARoF transport over 77 km of commercial fibre
- Successful spectral coexistence of DCO, mmWave, and sub-THz ARoF on a
  live production network
- Automatic equalisation enabling flexible multi-service provisioning
- New benchmark for ARoF field deployment beyond laboratory distances
- Recognised as a Top-Scored paper at OFC 2025
- Invited journal extension published in JOCN 2025
