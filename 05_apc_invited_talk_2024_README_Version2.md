# Hybrid Digital PAM and 60 GHz ARoF for OSaaS — Invited Talk, Optica APC 2024

**Hybrid Digital PAM and 60 GHz Analog Radio over Fiber Optical Spectrum
as a Service Applications**

Invited Talk — Optica Advanced Photonics Congress (APC), 2024
Photonic Networks and Devices (PND) Track

---

## Overview

This invited contribution presented a hybrid optical transmission architecture
combining digital PAM signals and 60 GHz analog radio-over-fiber (ARoF) within
an Optical Spectrum as a Service (OSaaS) framework. The work demonstrated how
flexible spectral management and software-controlled optical spectrum allocation
can support simultaneous delivery of heterogeneous digital and wireless services
over a shared optical infrastructure.

This work was a direct precursor to the live network field trial subsequently
presented at OFC 2025, establishing the experimental and conceptual framework
for multi-service OSaaS-controlled deployment over commercial fibre.

---

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated coexistence of digital PAM and 60 GHz ARoF under OSaaS control
- Validated the OSaaS framework as a practical mechanism for flexible
  multi-service optical spectrum allocation
- Established the experimental foundation for subsequent live network deployment
  over 77 km of commercial fibre
- Presented as an invited talk, reflecting community recognition of the
  research direction
