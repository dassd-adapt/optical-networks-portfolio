# InP-Si3N4 PIC and 28 GBd PAM-8 Transmission — Optics Express 2021

**28 GBd PAM-8 Transmission over a 100 nm Range using an InP-Si3N4
Based Integrated Dual Tunable Laser Module**

Optics Express, vol. 29, no. 11, pp. 16563-16571, 2021
Open Access

---

## Research Challenge

The central question was whether a widely tunable, low-noise integrated
dual-laser PIC could enable high-speed IM/DD links without optical
amplification or complex DSP — providing a scalable, cost-effective
path toward DWDM data-centre interconnects.

---

## Approach

- Characterised the InP-Si3N4 integrated dual tunable laser module across
  wavelength tuning range, side-mode suppression ratio (SMSR), and relative
  intensity noise (RIN)
- Developed automated long-duration measurement software for high-fidelity
  optical and electrical characterisation
- Designed and implemented a PAM-8 transceiver in MATLAB
- Demonstrated unamplified 28 GBd PAM-8 transmission across a 100 nm
  tuning range
- Led manuscript preparation and experimental execution

---

## Key Results

- 28 GBd PAM-8 transmission over a 100 nm tuning range, unamplified
- Low DSP complexity, practical for cost-sensitive deployment scenarios
- Demonstrated a scalable path to PIC-based DWDM solutions for data-centre
  interconnects
- Results directly supported the commercialisation pathway of the PIC design,
  adopted by a silicon photonics company for product development
