## PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

Experimental photonics researcher with 7+ years of expertise in silicon photonic integrated
circuits (Si-PICs), coherent optical transceivers, analog radio-over-fiber (ARoF), and
converged metro-access network testbeds. Demonstrated ability to lead end-to-end research
— from hypothesis and experimental design to field deployment and high-impact publication.
Author of a Top-Scored OFC 2025 paper reporting the world's first ARoF transmission over
77 km of commercial fibre. Experienced in MATLAB transceiver development, laboratory
automation, and Horizon Europe (SNS-JU) consortium research. Seeking to advance
next-generation photonic integration and 6G-ready optical transport at Fraunhofer HHI.
