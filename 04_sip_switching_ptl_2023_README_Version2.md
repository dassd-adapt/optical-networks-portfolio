# Ultra-Flexible Si-PIC Switching for mmWave OCDM and ARoF — IEEE PTL 2023

**Low Noise Ultra-Flexible SiP Switching Platform for mmWave OCDM
and Multi-band OFDM ARoF Fronthaul**

IEEE Photonics Technology Letters, vol. 35, no. 12, pp. 653-656, 2023

---

## Research Challenge

This work investigated whether a single silicon photonic switching platform
could simultaneously support mmWave OCDM and multi-band OFDM ARoF fronthaul,
enabling programmable, converged optical-wireless access networks with reduced
hardware complexity.

---

## Approach

- Demonstrated path-switched ARoF on a low-noise silicon photonics platform
- Introduced orthogonal chirp-division multiplexing (OCDM) over a SiP
  switching platform
- Enabled simultaneous transmission of PAM, 5G NR, and Wi-Fi compatible
  signals using digital multiplexing
- Characterised low-noise performance across all service types

---

## Key Results

- First demonstration of OCDM over a SiP switching platform
- Simultaneous delivery of PAM, 5G NR, and Wi-Fi compatible signals
  on a single optical infrastructure
- Reduced hardware complexity demonstrated through digital multiplexing
- Validated a programmable, converged optical-wireless access architecture
