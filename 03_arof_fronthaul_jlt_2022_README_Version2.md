# Flexible ARoF Fronthaul and mm-wave Generation — JLT 2022

**Wavelength and mm-wave Flexible Converged Optical Fronthaul with a
Low Noise Si-Based Integrated Dual Laser Source**

Journal of Lightwave Technology, vol. 40, no. 10, pp. 3307-3315, 2022
Open Access
Shortlisted — Best Student Paper, ECOC 2022 and MWP 2022

---

## Research Challenge

This work addressed whether a low-noise Si-based integrated dual laser source
could enable dynamic wavelength assignment and remote mm-wave generation in
a flexible converged optical fronthaul architecture — meeting key capacity and
flexibility requirements for 5G and 6G transport networks.

---

## Approach

- Designed and built the full experimental ARoF fronthaul testbed
- Demonstrated dynamic wavelength and RF carrier assignment using the
  Si-based dual tunable laser module
- Implemented remote mm-wave generation via optical heterodyning
- Developed a custom OFDM transceiver in MATLAB
- Performed measurements with multiple receiver configurations
- Led manuscript preparation and figure creation

---

## Key Results

- Dynamic wavelength and mm-wave carrier assignment demonstrated
  experimentally
- Remote mm-wave generation via heterodyne detection validated
- Experimentally verified route toward high-capacity, flexible
  optical-wireless links for 5G and 6G transport
- Shortlisted for Best Student Paper at ECOC 2022 and MWP 2022
